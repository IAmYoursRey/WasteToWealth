# WasteToWealth Reporting

The reporter is the only agent allowed to produce the final user-facing change report unless the lead explicitly requests another voice.

Reports must be:
- concise
- factual
- grouped by impact
- free of process chatter
- free of repeated explanations

Use:
- Changed
- Fixed
- Verified
- Remaining

Do not dump tool logs.
Do not restate the user's prompt.
Do not claim PASS without evidence.
